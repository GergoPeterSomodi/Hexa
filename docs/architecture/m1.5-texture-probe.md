# M1.5 Texture Memory & Image Format Probe (G1)

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Spec:** `docs/architecture/ui-and-controls-spec.md` §6.3  
**Plan:** `docs/architecture/ui-and-controls-plan.md` (Tasks G1 & U17)  
**Engine:** Godot `4.7.2-stable`, `gl_compatibility` (OpenGL 4.1 Compatibility on macOS)  
**Probe tool:** `godot/tools/probes/probe_textures.gd`

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Observed Apple M2 Pro Baseline

All 60 seasonal tile atlases (`spring`, `summer`, `autumn`, `winter_snow`, `winter_frost`, `winter_mild`) are imported with `compress/mode=2` (VRAM Compressed) and `compress/high_quality=true` (`BPTC_RGBA` / BC7, 1 byte/px). On Windows, GPUs accept `BPTC_RGBA` directly (~72.58 MB per spring/summer/autumn season, ~191.48 MB for winter).

Running `godot --path godot -s res://tools/probes/probe_textures.gd` windowed on `macOS / gl_compatibility / Apple M2 Pro` produced:

- **Baseline:** `RENDER_TEXTURE_MEM_USED=10.88 MB, RENDER_VIDEO_MEM_USED=16.99 MB`
- **Warnings on every atlas load in `TilesetBuilder.load_season`:**
  ```text
  WARNING: Image format BPTC_RGBA not supported by hardware, converting to RGBA8.
       at: _get_gl_image_and_format (drivers/gles3/storage/texture_storage.cpp:987)
  ```
- **Measured Full (1.0×) numbers:**

| Measurement | Delta Tex Mem (`RENDER_TEXTURE_MEM_USED`) | Delta Video Mem (`RENDER_VIDEO_MEM_USED`) | Unique Atlases | Actual Unpacked `RGBA8` Est (MB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `spring` | 96.88 MB | 96.88 MB | 10 | 290.33 MB |
| `summer` | 96.88 MB | 96.88 MB | 10 | 290.33 MB |
| `autumn` | 96.88 MB | 96.88 MB | 10 | 290.33 MB |
| `winter` | 255.54 MB | 255.54 MB | 23 | 765.91 MB |
| `summer + autumn` (preload ON) | 193.76 MB | 193.76 MB | 20 | 580.66 MB |
| `winter + spring` (preload ON) | 352.42 MB | 352.42 MB | 33 | **1056.24 MB** |
| Menu art (`menu_bg + menu_shade`, Lossless) | 11.20 MB | — | 2 | 8.40 MB |

> **Why `RENDER_TEXTURE_MEM_USED` reports ~1/3 of the `RGBA8` estimate for `BPTC_RGBA`:**  
> Godot's GLES3 `TextureStorage` computes `RENDER_TEXTURE_MEM_USED` from the imported `CompressedTexture2D` source size (`BPTC_RGBA` at 1 byte/px, scaled by 4/3 in the engine's texture-memory accounting: `72.58 × 4/3 ≈ 96.88 MB`), *before* `_get_gl_image_and_format` decompresses `BPTC_RGBA` to `RGBA8` (4 bytes/px) on CPU and uploads **290.33 MB** (single season) / **765.91 MB** (winter) / **1056.24 MB** (winter + spring) to the macOS OpenGL driver. That matches the ~845–1,199 MB real VRAM footprint observed on macOS and breaks the M1 512 MB VRAM budget in winter.

## 2. Why `import_etc2_astc = true` Alone Does Not Solve macOS OpenGL 4.1

Enabling `rendering/textures/vram_compression/import_etc2_astc=true` in `godot/project.godot` causes `--import` to emit `.astc.ctex` alongside `.bptc.ctex` in every tile `.png.import` (`"imported_formats": ["s3tc_bptc", "etc2_astc"]`), while `export_presets.cfg` retains `texture_format/s3tc_bptc=true` and `texture_format/etc2_astc=false` so Windows builds remain BPTC-only.

However, testing on macOS `gl_compatibility` (OpenGL 4.1) confirmed:

1. **macOS OpenGL 4.1 lacks BPTC, ETC2, and ASTC:** Apple froze desktop OpenGL at 4.1 (`GL_ARB_texture_compression_bptc` requires GL 4.2+; `GL_ARB_ES3_compatibility`/ETC2 requires GL 4.3+; `GL_KHR_texture_compression_astc_ldr` is GLES 3.2/Metal/Vulkan only). Direct `Image.compress()` probes under macOS `gl_compatibility` confirmed:
   - `BPTC_RGBA`: `WARNING: Image format BPTC_RGBA not supported by hardware, converting to RGBA8.`
   - `ETC2_RGBA8`: `WARNING: Image format ETC2_RGBA8 not supported by hardware, converting to RGBA8.`
   - `ASTC_4x4`: `WARNING: Image format ASTC_4x4 not supported by hardware, converting to RGBA8.`
   - Only `DXT1`/`DXT5` (`S3TC`, `GL_EXT_texture_compression_s3tc`) uploads without fallback, which introduces unacceptable block artifacts on painted terrain gradients.
2. **Desktop GL loader format selection:** Even with `etc2_astc` imported alongside `s3tc_bptc`, desktop GLES3 `CompressedTexture2D` selects `.bptc.ctex` and falls back to `RGBA8` with the exact same `96.88 MB` / `352.42 MB` (`1056.24 MB` `RGBA8` est) footprint and warnings.

## 3. Half-Resolution Atlases (`0.5×`) & `size_override` Mechanism Trial

### 3.1 `size_override` vs Scaled `TileMapLayer` Trial (Godot 4.7.2)

We tested both candidate mechanisms from `ui-and-controls-spec.md` §6.3 in Godot 4.7.2:

1. **`PortableCompressedTexture2D.size_override`:** In Godot 4.7.2, setting `pct.size_override = Vector2(128, 128)` on a `64×64` `PortableCompressedTexture2D` does **not** change `pct.get_size()` (`(64, 64)`), so `TileSetAtlasSource.create_tile` fails with `ERROR: Cannot create tile. The tile is outside the texture`.
2. **`ImageTexture.set_size_override`:** Overrides `get_size()` to `(128, 128)` so `TileSetAtlasSource.create_tile` succeeds, **but** `TileMapLayer` rendering in `gl_compatibility` still draws the tile quad at the underlying texture pixel dimensions (`64×64` instead of `128×128`), leaving the right/bottom halves blank.
3. **Half `TileSet`s (`tile_size = 52×51`, halved `region`/`separation`/`texture_origin`/`y_sort_origin`) drawn on seasonal `TileMapLayer`s with `scale = Vector2(2, 2)`:**
   - All 10 atlas sources in `index.json` have even image sizes, even region sizes (`172×170`, `252×216`, `76×84`, `236×150`, `120×120`), and `separation = 2`.
   - `Vector2i(104, 102) / 2 = Vector2i(52, 51)` is exact (`0.75 * 51 = 38.25`, `38.25 * 2 = 76.5 = MapTiles.ROW`), so `TileMapView.hex_at` and `centre_of` match full resolution 1:1.
   - `Image.INTERPOLATE_BILINEAR` 2× downsampling produces **0** non-zero alpha pixels in the 1 px atlas separators.
   - Cross-layer `y_sort_enabled` inside `Standing` (`Node2D`) sorts tiles identically across `scale = Vector2(2, 2)` layers.
   - `grid`, `marks` (using shared 1× `_marks_set`), and `Lines` (`EdgeLineLayer` river/road meshes) remain at `scale = Vector2(1, 1)` for sharp vector/grid lines.

Running `probe_textures.gd` windowed after generating 0.5× atlases (`19 MB` on disk across 60 PNGs) and `tiles_<season>_half.tres`:

| Measurement | Delta Tex Mem (`RENDER_TEXTURE_MEM_USED`) | Delta Video Mem (`RENDER_VIDEO_MEM_USED`) | Unique Atlases | Actual `RGBA8` VRAM (MB) | `BPTC_RGBA` Warnings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `spring` (Half) | 96.75 MB | 96.75 MB | 10 | **72.58 MB** | **0** |
| `summer` (Half) | 96.75 MB | 96.75 MB | 10 | **72.58 MB** | **0** |
| `autumn` (Half) | 96.75 MB | 96.75 MB | 10 | **72.58 MB** | **0** |

## 4. Recommendation for U17

1. **Keep `textures/vram_compression/import_etc2_astc=true` in `godot/project.godot`** so ASTC/ETC2 variants are generated alongside BPTC (while `export_presets.cfg` keeps Windows Desktop BPTC-only).
2. **Ship Half-resolution (`0.5×`) tile atlases and `tiles_<season>_half.tres`** generated deterministically by `godot/tools/build_tileset.gd` (with `--half` and `--check` verification in CI), rendered by `TileMapView` at `scale = Vector2(2, 2)` on seasonal layers.
3. **Promote `graphics/texture_quality` (`["full", "half"]`) and `graphics/preset` (`["low", "medium", "high", "custom"]`) to M1.5** in `Settings` and `Options`:
   - Default `graphics/texture_quality` to `"half"` on `"macOS"` (and `"full"` on Windows/Linux), keeping `graphics/preload_next_season = true` because `winter + spring` in Half is 264.06 MB (< 512 MB).
   - Default `graphics/preset` to `"custom"` on `"macOS"` (Half + Relief shading + Preload) and `"medium"` on Windows/Linux (Full + Relief shading + Preload).
