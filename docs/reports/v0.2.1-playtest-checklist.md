# v0.2.1 playtest checklist (war you can follow)

For the owner. About 30 minutes, one sitting. The brief is `docs/roadmap/release-0.2.1-brief.md`; the rules are in
`README.md` (Age of Discovery, War).

Start as a mid-sized country in Age of Discovery (Europe 1480 is fine). Keep a note of the season for anything odd.

Judge this release on "can I follow the war, and does losing hurt". It adds no new war decision; those come with sieges
in 0.2.2.

| # | Try this | Pass looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Play five seasons without going to war. Watch the "N things to do" flag under End season (or left of an open panel). | End season never opens a pop-up. The flag's count matches what is left; a click opens the list, a line takes you there, and the count drops when you do it. Fund research is on the list whenever you can buy it. |
| 2 | Declare war and attack an enemy stack at "Even" or "Favourable" odds. End the season. | The battle ends in that same season: the log line says who won and in how many days ("won in 4 days"). Nothing is left on the hex for next season. |
| 3 | Click the battle's log line. | The battle panel opens on the ended battle with its day log. You can say why it went the way it did. |
| 4 | Attack with two stacks on the same enemy hex in one season. | The second stack joins the battle on day 1 (it is in the panel's lanes or reserve). |
| 5 | Give a weak stack the Withdraw order (hover it first), then let it be attacked. | The hover shows both prices before you choose. The army fights at least 2 days, then leaves in order, paying 5% of its men (10% as attacker) and 25 morale. |
| 6 | Lose a battle with an army that does not withdraw. | It flees when its morale drops under 10%: it loses about 15% of its men and its guns, and cannot attack for about 2 seasons. It runs to your own land if any is near, not onto the enemy's. |
| 7 | Hover an attack on hills or in a forest, then on plains, with the same stacks. | The odds word is lower on hills and forests. The word matches what then happens more often than not. |
| 8 | Save during a war, quit to the menu and load it. | The game loads, and the flag and the log are as you left them. |
| 9 | Play on for ten seasons of war. Note anything that felt unfair, slow or silent, with the season. | Losing a battle hurts but does not end your war at once; a beaten AI comes back within a few years. |

## Blocking answers

A "no" on steps 2, 5, 6 or 8 blocks the release. Everything else becomes an issue or a note for 0.2.2.

## What to write down

For each "no": the step, the season, what you expected and what happened. Also: did war feel easier to follow than in
v0.2.0, and did losing feel like it cost something?
