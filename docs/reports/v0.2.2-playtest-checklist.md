# v0.2.2 playtest checklist (sieges)

For the owner. About 40 minutes, one sitting. The brief is `docs/roadmap/release-0.2.2-brief.md`; the rules are in
`README.md` (Age of Discovery, War, Sieges).

Start as a mid-sized country next to a neighbour with castles (Portugal or Castile in Europe 1480 is fine). Keep a note of
the season for anything odd.

This is the release that should make war a decision: besiege slowly and cheaply, or storm fast and dear; relieve a place or
let it fall.

| # | Try this | Pass looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Declare war. Select a stack of 3 or more regiments and hover an enemy castle next to it. | The odds card shows "Besiege: falls in about N seasons" and, below, a word for Storm. It warns you if the war ends first. |
| 2 | Right-click the castle (Besiege). End seasons until it falls. | A dial badge sits on the castle and fills each season; its hover says how far it is, how fast it goes and how long the stores last. The place holds at least 2 seasons. When the stores run out, "starving" shows and the meter speeds up. The log names the siege ("Siege of X, 1480"). |
| 3 | Besiege another castle; after two or three seasons, open its province and press "Storm now". | The storm is a battle against a garrison already weakened by the siege; it costs fewer men than storming on arrival would. |
| 4 | Hold Shift and right-click a fresh castle (Storm on arrival). | An assault battle starts at once, at its full cost. |
| 5 | Try to besiege a big castle with one regiment. | It is refused with a reason ("Too few men..."); Shift still lets you storm. |
| 6 | Let the AI besiege one of your places. | Your task flag says "X is under siege: falls in about N seasons". The province panel shows the siege and "Relief: your army ... can reach it in N seasons"; a click selects that army. |
| 7 | Send the relief army and win the battle. | The siege is lifted: a grey flash on the badge and a log line. The besieger starts from zero if it comes back. |
| 8 | Make peace with a third country while a siege against your enemy is on. | The siege goes on (only peace with the place's holder ends it). |
| 9 | Save mid-siege, quit to the menu and load. | The badge, meter and seasons are as you left them, and the siege plays on. |
| 10 | Play ten more seasons of war. | Land still changes hands; sieges feel like a choice, not a wait. Note any siege that stalled or any AI army that stood still for many seasons. |

## Blocking answers

A "no" on steps 2, 3, 6 or 9 blocks the release. Everything else becomes an issue or a note for the next AI pass.

## What to write down

For each "no": the step, the season, what you expected and what happened. Also: did war feel more engaging than in 0.2.1?
Did besiege-or-storm feel like a real choice?
