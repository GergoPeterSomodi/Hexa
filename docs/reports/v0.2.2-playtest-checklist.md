# v0.2.2 playtest checklist (sieges and castle areas)

For the owner. About 45 minutes, one sitting. The brief is `docs/roadmap/release-0.2.2-brief.md`; the rules are in
`README.md` (Age of Discovery, War, Sieges, Castle areas).

Start as a mid-sized country next to a neighbour with castles (Castile against Granada or Portugal in Europe 1480 is fine).
Keep a note of the season for anything odd.

This is the release that should make war a decision: break the castle, then sweep its land; besiege slowly and cheaply, or
storm fast and dear; relieve a place or let it fall. Wars are slower than before, on purpose.

| # | Try this | Pass looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Declare war. Select an army. | The enemy's castles show the land they hold: a dashed line and a light tint. Nothing shows when no army at war is selected. |
| 2 | Hover and click a province inside an outlined area, then one outside it. | The panel says "Held by the castle of X" (its hover shows the path to the castle) or "Open land: no castle holds it". A castle's own panel says how many provinces it holds. |
| 3 | Move an army onto open enemy land and end the season. | It is yours at the end of that season. |
| 4 | Move an army onto held land. | The card says it moves in but cannot take it while the castle stands. The land stays the enemy's. |
| 5 | Hover a castle with an army of 3 or more regiments beside it. | The odds card shows "Besiege: falls in about N seasons", a word for Storm, and "Taking it opens N provinces". |
| 6 | Right-click the castle (Besiege). End seasons until it falls. | The dial badge fills each season; the castle holds at least 2 seasons; "starving" shows when the stores run out. The log names the siege and says the castle's land lies open. The next season your armies can take its land, one province per army per season. |
| 7 | Besiege another castle; after two or three seasons, press "Storm now" in its province panel. Also Shift-right-click a fresh castle. | A late storm meets a weakened garrison and costs fewer men than a storm on arrival. A stormed castle's land opens the next season, as after a siege. |
| 8 | Hover hexes you cannot reach, or reach only slowly. | Each says why in words before you click: a slow march names the slow unit and the ground ("About 2 seasons away: ..."), a halt beside an enemy castle names it, a full stack says how many are there. A right-click on a refused hex shows the same text. |
| 9 | Let the AI besiege one of your castles. | The task flag says it is under siege; the panel shows the siege and which of your armies can relieve it; a relief that wins lifts the siege. |
| 10 | Save mid-siege, quit and load. | The siege, the badge and the areas are as you left them. |
| 11 | Play ten more seasons of war. | Land still changes hands, more slowly; you can tell from the map and the cards why an army can or cannot take a place. Note any AI army that stands still for many seasons. |

## Blocking answers

A "no" on steps 1, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 10 blocks the release. Everything else becomes an issue or a note for the castles map
mode and the next AI pass.

## What to write down

For each "no": the step, the season, what you expected and what happened. Also: did war feel more engaging than in 0.2.1?
Did "take the castle, then its land" read on the map?
