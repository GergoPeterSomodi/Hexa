# v0.2.0 playtest checklist (battles, slice 1)

For the owner, or anyone who did not build the battle system. About 40 minutes, one sitting. Rules are in
`README.md` (Age of Discovery, War) and the review's release gate is `docs/design/battle-plan-review.md` 3.3 and 3.4.

Start as a mid-sized country in Age of Discovery (Europe 1480 is fine). Keep a note of the season for anything odd.

Pass means: the step's answer is "yes" without help from this document or the source code.

| # | Try this | Pass looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Declare a war on a neighbour. Select a stack and hover an enemy hex it can attack. | The odds card shows one word (Hopeless, Poor, Even, Favourable or Overwhelming), the men and morale of each side, and the ground. You believe the word: a stack that is "Poor" looks like the loser. |
| 2 | Attack at "Even" odds. Watch for two seasons (Clash timing, the default). | Within one look at the map you can say who is winning: the battle's marker or badge shows the side that holds the hex and its strength. |
| 3 | Open the battle panel during the fight. | Without help you can name your men, the wounded, your morale, and one reason you are winning or losing (a lane broken, a flank, the reserve, the ground). |
| 4 | Switch your stack to Hold, then to Withdraw (the battle orders). | Hold keeps the fight on the hex. Withdraw pulls the army back to the nearest hex of your country within 3 hexes, or surrenders if there is none. The cost (men lost on the way out) is shown before you choose. |
| 5 | Wait for the AI to attack one of your stacks. Note the season. | Clash: the first day is fought in the attack's season, so you get a turn to answer (orders, a second stack joining) before most men die. If men died before you could answer, that is a "no". |
| 6 | Send a second stack onto your own battle hex (or a hex beside it that holds a battle). | It joins the side and you can see it in the panel, either in the lanes or in the reserve. |
| 7 | Attack a castle with a garrison. | The battle reads as an assault (the card and the panel say so). The castle's garrison is shown as men. The result shows what happened to the castle (held, taken or still under siege). |
| 8 | Lose a battle. Note how many seasons your beaten army needs before it can attack again. | The game says why it cannot attack yet (its morale, or the 50% line). The army recovers over a few seasons outside battle. |
| 9 | Save in the middle of a battle (Esc, then save), quit to the menu, load the save. | The same battle is on the same hex with the same men, morale and orders. After one more season the battle has moved on, not restarted. If the numbers differ, that is a "no" and blocks the release. |
| 10 | Open Options and set "Battle timing" to Quick battles, then fight a battle. | Quick battles fights all of a season's days at once: the battle ends sooner, and the panel names the timing. Clash is still the default in a new game. |
| 11 | Watch for the end-of-turn reminder "... is still on: join, withdraw or fight on" and the "Battle on" ribbon at the start of a season. | Both appear for each battle you are in that is still on, and a click on the reminder opens the battle panel. A battle you have left does not show. |
| 12 | Read the log after ten seasons. Note anything that felt unfair, slow or silent, with the season. | You can name one battle, its place and its year (for example "Battle of Toro, 1480"; a repeat is "Second Battle of Toro, 1481"). |

## Blocking answers

A "no" on steps 2, 3, 5 or 9 blocks the release (the review's gate). Steps 1 and 7 are "no" when the word or the
assault reading is clearly wrong; file an issue with the season and the two sides. Everything else becomes an issue.

## What to write down

For each step: yes, no or partly, and the season. For a "no", the one thing you expected that did not happen.
